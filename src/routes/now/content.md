## AI, and the people building with it

I’m working on [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/), [AI Engineer](https://ai.engineer/), and [Cognition](/cognition), based in San Francisco.

Latent Space is where I write and have conversations about AI and the people building it. AI Engineer is where those conversations meet a community of practitioners. For more about my work at Cognition, [start here](/cognition).

## Giving this site some attention

I’m revisiting this site’s design and organization: a more personal About page, a library that’s easier to browse, and a better home for talks, podcasts, and photos.

I want it to feel like a public notebook you can spend time in. [Learn in Public](/learn-in-public) is still a good introduction to that idea. The [Coding Career Handbook](https://learninpublic.org/) is also now free and open source.
